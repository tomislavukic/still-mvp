Trust & privacy

Privacy without a data appetite.

Still? is designed to answer a practical consumer question without requiring an account or turning your receipt into an advertising profile.

What stays on your device

Return checks, recent checks, language and appearance preferences are stored in your browser. Receipt images used by the current on-device scanning feature are not intentionally uploaded to a Still? server.

Keep sensitive information out.

Do not enter names, addresses, payment details or other sensitive personal information into the optional item field.

Shared result links

A result link can contain purchase details that you choose to include so the recipient can reopen the same calculation. Review the result and item description before sharing the link publicly.

Analytics

Still? does not need behavioural advertising to work. If privacy-preserving aggregate traffic analytics are enabled by the site operator, this notice should be updated with the provider and data practices before commercial launch.

Data minimisation

The product should collect only what is necessary for the feature being used. If future features introduce server-side receipt processing, accounts, email reminders or cloud history, this privacy notice must be updated before those features are enabled.

Contact

A working privacy/support contact address must be published here before commercial launch.

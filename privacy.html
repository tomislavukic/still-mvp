Privacy

Still? is designed to work without an account.

Checks and recent history

Purchase-check data and recent checks are stored in your browser so the tool can work without creating an account. Clearing browser site data removes that local history.

Receipts

Receipt import is intended to use browser or device recognition when that capability is available. Recognition is a convenience only. You must review suggested details before relying on them.

Sharing

Sharing happens only when you choose a share action. The destination service may process information under its own privacy policy.

Important limitation

Do not treat Still? as secure archival storage for receipts or purchase records. Keep your original receipt and other evidence separately.

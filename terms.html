Terms & disclaimer

Still? is an informational calculator intended to help you understand possible return windows and consumer warranty periods.

Not legal advice

Results are not legal advice and do not create or determine legal rights. Laws, retailer policies, exclusions, product categories and individual circumstances can change the outcome.

Verify before acting

Where an official source is provided, check that source before relying on a deadline. Retailer return policies are different from statutory consumer rights.

Dates

The accuracy of a result depends on the date and purchase information you enter. Delivery date rather than order date may matter for some distance purchases.

No invented certainty

When Still? does not have a verified rule for a situation, it should not invent one. A missing answer is preferable to a false deadline.

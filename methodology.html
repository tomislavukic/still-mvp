Still?

Sources & methodology

Still? separates two different things: statutory consumer rights created by law and voluntary return policies offered by retailers.

Source hierarchy

  1. Official government or EU consumer-rights sources for statutory rights.
  2. Official retailer policy pages for retailer-specific rules.
  3. User-entered custom periods only when the user chooses the custom calculator.

No invented deadlines

If a fixed retailer deadline cannot be established from a sufficiently clear official source, Still? should not invent one. The result instead directs the user to verify the current official policy.

Dates

Where a policy has been reviewed, Still? may display a last-reviewed date. A review date is not a promise that the policy has not changed since then.

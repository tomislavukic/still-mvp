Sources & methodology

Still? deliberately separates statutory consumer rights from voluntary retailer policies.

Return calculations

The calculator uses the selected market, purchase method, date and the verified rule associated with the selected source. A retailer policy can be more generous than the legal minimum, but it does not replace statutory rights.

Warranty guidance

For EU markets, Still? presents the standard minimum legal-guarantee framework as general guidance and links to official EU consumer information. National rules and individual circumstances can provide different or additional remedies.

Source hierarchy

Official government, EU and retailer policy pages are preferred. Still? should expose an official verification link when a result depends on a rule that users may need to act on.

Conservative behavior

If a rule cannot be supported confidently, Still? should ask for a custom window or direct the user to an official source rather than fabricate a deadline.

# Still v1.0.0 — Everything you own

Still v1.0.0 is the first complete ownership operating-system release. It moves the product beyond a return and warranty checker into one authenticated environment for remembering, understanding, resolving and safely transferring the things a person owns.

## Buyer World

- Durable server-side Things built on the existing ownership passport identity.
- Private receipt and document storage in Cloudflare R2 with authenticated delivery.
- Receipt review, duplicate protection, evidence provenance and Product Passport history.
- Knowledge, Situations, Open Loops, universal search and deterministic Now priorities.
- Idempotent migration of supported legacy browser ownership data.

## Still OS

- One responsive authenticated environment for Now, World, Discover and Together.
- Deep-linked workspaces for Things, Knowledge, Situations, Needs, listings, offers and deals.
- Mobile-first navigation, accessible controls, reduced-motion support and English/Croatian presentation over the same canonical data.

## Needs and resolution

- Persisted Needs with ownership-scoped relationships to Things, Knowledge and Situations.
- Deterministic World-first resolution that explains evidence and limitations.
- Real user-entered quotes, explicit selection, Handle It workflows and attributable resolution outcomes.
- No fabricated providers, prices, availability or simulated successful outcomes.

## Ownership-native Still Market

- Listings reference the same canonical Thing rather than creating a second product record.
- Structured condition disclosure and explainable Product Trust and Seller Trust components.
- Public-safe, paginated search with price, condition, delivery and trust-evidence filters.
- Persisted Wanted Objects, deterministic matching and opt-in private reverse matching.
- Structured offers, immutable counteroffer history, participant-only deals and messaging.
- Honest external-payment and pickup/external-shipping boundaries—Still does not claim escrow or carrier tracking.
- Ordered two-party handoff and ownership confirmation.
- Privacy-filtered transfer of the same Thing to its new owner while raw receipts, private notes, documents, Knowledge, addresses and personal relationships remain with the seller.
- Completed transfers update World, Product Passport, History, notifications and linked Need outcomes.

## Still for Business

CompanyOS remains fully available to authenticated business users. Existing company verification, operational tools, commerce, lifecycle, rewards, passport, service and Trust Layer functionality is preserved behind its separate authentication boundary.

## Deliberate boundaries

Still v1.0.0 does not invent capabilities that are not backed by production infrastructure. C2C payment remains external/manual; delivery is pickup or externally arranged; no escrow, payment protection, carrier tracking, AI condition inspection, professional inspection, market valuation or seller-rating claims are enabled.

Company and buyer authentication remain separate. Existing backend APIs, D1 identifiers, R2 privacy boundaries and production data compatibility are preserved.
